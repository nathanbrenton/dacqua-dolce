# PT17 Client Follow-Up Questions

These questions are intended to gather the next round of authoritative product, sales, installation, and customer-experience direction without forcing premature decisions.

## Friendly visual review reminder

When you have a few minutes, please try the live site in both **Light and Dark appearance**, and cycle through the available **themes and font/typography choices**. We have intentionally made the font choices feel meaningfully different while keeping their responsive size hierarchy consistent. Any combinations that feel especially strong—or that feel less aligned with D'Acqua Dolce—would be very helpful to know.

## Product and catalog

1. **Refine softener line:** Do you now have an authoritative model/SKU list, manufacturer specifications, media/resin information, valve/control details, or approved product descriptions for the conventional softener family?
2. **Refine sizing:** Do you have confirmed rules for choosing between capacities such as 1.5 cu. ft. and 2.0 cu. ft.? If so, which inputs control sizing: bathrooms, occupants, service-pipe size, peak flow, measured hardness, iron/manganese, or something else?
3. **Harmony sizing:** Are there authoritative sizing/capacity rules for Harmony that we can publish or use internally, or should Harmony recommendations continue to stop at human review when sizing is required?
4. **Harmony technical documentation:** Can you provide the manufacturer or technical documentation describing the calcium/magnesium interaction or crystal-formation mechanism that we intentionally have not published yet?
5. **Duo cartridge filtration:** Do you have the exact products/SKUs, cartridge sequence, micron ratings, carbon type, dimensions, pressure-drop information, and replacement specifications for the preferred sediment + carbon Duo arrangement?
6. **Single cartridge filtration:** Should there also be a formally offered Single cartridge configuration? If yes, what is its intended use and exact cartridge specification?
7. **UV option:** Do you have an authoritative UV product/model and technical specifications that can be associated with Harmony or other systems?
8. **Essence:** Are there additional verified specifications or approved public claims for the backwashing carbon system that we can safely publish beyond the conservative wording currently used?
9. **Origin / RO:** Are there any changes you want to make to the Origin and Origin Alkaline product positioning, included stages, remineralization language, or maintenance expectations?
10. **Product documents:** Which authoritative files are ready first: specification sheet, owner's manual, installation guide, maintenance guide, warranty, service schedule, or water-test/report information?

## Recommendation and water testing

11. For **well water**, should the required third-party laboratory testing follow a preferred panel or list of analytes before D'Acqua Dolce provides a system recommendation?
12. For **municipal water**, would you like customers to be encouraged to upload or provide their local water-quality report when available, or only when a recommendation requires additional review?
13. Are there any additional conditions that should always trigger **human review** rather than an automated recommendation?
14. Should chlorine and chloramine be treated differently in the recommendation flow once reliable water-report information is available?
15. Do you want the recommendation result to become more specific once verified sizing rules are supplied—for example, recommending a family plus a capacity/configuration rather than only treatment components?

## Installation and ownership

16. For the initial equipment-only launch, what installation information should customers receive immediately after purchase: preparation checklist, plumbing/electrical requirements, bypass guidance, installer checklist, or something else?
17. For irrigation, hose bibs, and pool-fill lines, are there any situations where you specifically **do** want them treated rather than bypassed?
18. Do you have preferred wording for explaining that pool-fill bypass helps preserve filtration-media service life?
19. For softeners, can the proposed salt-level guidance be made more specific, or should we continue with the conservative monthly-inspection/replenish wording until manufacturer confirmation?
20. For backwashing carbon and softener valves, is checking the time-of-day after a power interruption sufficient, or are there other customer checks you want included?

## Availability, inventory, and fulfillment

21. Who will maintain estimated lead times in Operations, and how precise should customer-facing lead-time language be: exact dates, week ranges, or broad wording such as “typically ships in 2–3 weeks”?
22. When an out-of-stock item becomes available, should **Notify When in Stock** send one transactional message and automatically close that subscription?
23. Should customers be able to cancel an availability notification request from their account before the item returns to stock?
24. Are there products that should remain visible while unavailable, and others that should be temporarily hidden from the public catalog?
25. Do you expect inventory to represent complete systems only, or also individual cartridges, replacement media, UV lamps, and service parts?

## Direct purchase and payments

26. For the initial direct-purchase launch, which product family should be the first one customers can actually purchase online?
27. Do you have the **Affinity 24** integration documentation we can review, including sandbox/test credentials, hosted-payment or API flow, webhook documentation, signature verification, refund/void behavior, and idempotency guidance?
28. Will pricing generally be visible publicly, or are there products/brands where MAP or manufacturer policy requires different presentation?
29. At launch, should checkout support shipping only, local pickup, installer delivery, or some combination?
30. Are taxes, shipping charges, and installation charges expected to be calculated separately from equipment pricing?

## Customer account, maintenance, and follow-up

31. Which maintenance reminder should we activate first once authoritative service intervals are available: cartridge replacement, softener salt/check, UV lamp/service, annual system review, or another reminder?
32. Should post-purchase and post-installation follow-ups ask for specific information—for example installation date, installer, serial number, water-test result, or customer satisfaction?
33. Do you want installed-equipment records to be customer-editable for fields such as serial number and installation date, or employee-managed only?
34. When QR codes are introduced on equipment, should they lead to a public product-support page or directly into the signed-in customer's equipment record?
35. Are there warranty-registration steps or manufacturer registrations we should eventually incorporate into the account experience?

## Sales and Operations workflow

37. Would a simple **“Follow up by” date** be useful, or would that create unnecessary process for the current sales volume?
38. For Customer Orders, what real-world statuses do you expect employees to use from purchase through fulfillment and completion? We can model the actual workflow once you describe it in plain language.
39. Is there any operational information you regularly expect to see at a glance that is still missing from the compact Operations attention dashboard?
40. Are there any actions in Operations that currently take too many clicks or require scrolling/searching when they should be immediately accessible?

## Brand and presentation

45. Does the wellness-oriented presentation feel clear and calm without becoming generic?
46. Are there any areas where the site still feels too dense, too sparse, too technical, or too “marketing-heavy”?
